# Verification log: P2-9-AR-DEVELOPMENT

Section: Argentina: the development of democracy (1983–1999).
There is no Argentina chapter in the course textbook, and the US Foreign Relations volume for Argentina in 1981–88 has not yet been released, so this section uses fetched pages named in the Source column with their URLs: Congressional Research Service reports RS20781 (2001), RS21072 (2003), RS21113 (2006) and R43816 (2017) at everycrsreport.com, for the overall narrative; and Wikipedia articles on the Trial of the Juntas, the Full Stop law, the Law of Due Obedience, the Carapintadas, the Austral plan, Carlos Menem, the Pact of Olivos and the 1994 constitutional amendment, for specific dates, laws and figures. Statements marked 'citation needed' on those pages were not used. All pages were fetched and checked on the date in the last column. Rows 25–28 support the comparative question on South Africa: they repeat checks recorded in `content/_verification/P2-9-SA-POLICIES.md` (rows F-SAP-03, -05, -06 and -09) against J. Bottaro and J. Stanley, *Evolution and Development of Democratic States (1848–2000)*, with the same line numbers and check date.

Not used because the sources conflict: how early Alfonsín left office (CRS RS21113: 'six months before his six-year term ended'; Wikipedia 'Carlos Menem': 'five months early, on 8 July'; the section gives the date, 8 July 1989, and 'five months', which matches the scheduled 10 December handover); the year of Menem's election (CRS RS21072 says 1990, against 1989 in CRS RS20781, RS21113 and R43816 and Wikipedia; 1989 is used); and when the pardons were granted (Wikipedia's 'National Reorganization Process' says 1989, 'Trial of the Juntas' says 1989–1990; the section follows the 'Carapintadas' article: October 1989 and December 1990). Also not used: vote counts in Congress for the Full Stop Law and the content of its Article 5, both marked 'citation needed'; the exact date of the Austral plan, which the page does not give.
