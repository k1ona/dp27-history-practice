# Verification log: section map (Stage 0)

Scope: wording of the legacy guide bullets, section titles and timeframe years used in `content/sections.json`.
Only checks that were actually performed are listed. Access date for every entry: **2026-11-06**.

**Method notes**
- The official legacy IB History guide is not in the project and was not found at a lawful public URL. Every "verified" entry below is therefore a **secondary reproduction** of the guide's wording on a teacher or school page. None is the guide itself.
- Entries marked *(local extraction)* were read from a downloaded PDF with `pypdf`, so the characters are as printed in the PDF. Entries marked *(fetch extraction)* were quoted through the web-fetch tool's text extraction, so their character-level fidelity (hyphens, accents) should be spot-checked against the official guide.
- One search result pointed to an unauthorised mirror of IB documents (guides and Questionbank pages). It was **not opened and not used**.
