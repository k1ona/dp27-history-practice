# Verification log: P2-12-SINO

Section: Sino-Soviet and Sino-US relations (1947–1979).
Rows citing the Office of the Historian were checked against its 'Milestones in the History of U.S. Foreign Relations' pages; rows citing FRUS were checked against documents in *Foreign Relations of the United States* on history.state.gov (US government publications, including declassified intelligence estimates of 1961, 1966 and 1969). All pages were downloaded on the access date and saved in the private project folder. Milestones paragraph numbers count body paragraphs, excluding image captions; FRUS locations use the document's own lettered conclusions or numbered paragraphs.

Not used: the Sino-Soviet Treaty of 1950, the exact date and place of the March 1969 fighting beyond 'the armed clashes on the Ussuri River last March', the month of the 1960 withdrawal of Soviet technicians and numbers of technicians, because none of the sources downloaded gives them (Britannica returned 'Forbidden' and the Wilson Center archive could not be reached, so they were not used). The intelligence estimates are cited as evidence of what US analysts believed at the time, not as neutral history; the content attributes their judgements to them.
